Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website and request a cleaning easily.

Our goal

This site is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. That includes:

  • A “skip to main content” link and clear page landmarks and headings
  • Full keyboard access, with a visible focus outline
  • Text and buttons with strong color contrast
  • Form fields with clear labels, grouped questions, and error messages that screen readers announce
  • Layouts that work when zoomed to 200% or more and on small screens
  • Respect for your device’s “reduce motion” setting
  • Decorative images and sparkles hidden from screen readers

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, please let us know and we’ll help you another way and work to fix it. Call or text 248-924-4573 or email hello@example.com. You can always request a quote by phone instead of the online form.
