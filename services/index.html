Common questions

Is the online estimate my final price?

No. The estimate is a starting point based on your answers. Every home is different, so Ashley confirms your final price with you before any cleaning is scheduled.

What’s the difference between routine and deep cleaning?

Routine cleaning keeps a home fresh with regular upkeep: kitchen, bathrooms, dusting, and floors. Deep cleaning goes further into the details, like baseboards, blinds, and built-up grime. Many homes start with a deep clean and then move to routine visits.

Do you offer recurring cleaning?

Yes. You can choose weekly, every 2 weeks, or every 4 weeks. Recurring routine cleaning comes with a discount on each visit.

Can I customize what gets cleaned?

Absolutely. Choose the rooms you want included and add extras like inside the oven or refrigerator, interior windows, or carpet shampoo. Tell Ashley about any areas you want extra focus on.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

It’s up to you. Let us know on the quote form whether you’ll be home, and Ashley will work out the details with you directly. Please don’t send door or alarm codes through the website.

How does the $25 referral discount work?

When someone you referred requests a quote, have them mention your name. Ask Ashley for the details on how the $25 off a cleaning is applied.

How do I reschedule?

Call or text 248-924-4573 as early as you can, and Ashley will help you find a new time.
